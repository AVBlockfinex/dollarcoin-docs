# DollarCoin (USDR)

**Technical Overview & Stablecoin Framework**  
**Version 1.0 | May 2026**

## Overview

DollarCoin (USDR) is a fully USD-backed stablecoin designed to provide instant, low-cost, and globally accessible digital U.S. dollars across multiple blockchain ecosystems.

USDR maintains a 1:1 peg to the U.S. Dollar, combining the stability of traditional financial reserves with the speed, transparency, and programmability of blockchain infrastructure.

Built for both institutional and retail use cases, DollarCoin enables efficient digital payments, remittances, treasury management, trading, and decentralized finance (DeFi) participation while prioritizing compliance, transparency, and reserve security.

## Core Principles

### Accessibility

USDR enables fast, borderless access to digital U.S. dollars, reducing friction in global transfers and financial operations.

### Regulation & Compliance

DollarCoin operates within a U.S.-regulated framework and is structured to adhere to applicable Money Services Business (MSB) and money transmitter compliance standards.

### Security

USDR reserves are maintained through secure financial infrastructure with segregated custody arrangements and conservative reserve management practices.

### Transparency

DollarCoin emphasizes transparency through on-chain verification, reserve reporting, and periodic third-party reserve attestations.

## Reserve Backing

DollarCoin is designed to be 100% reserve-backed, with every USDR token issued supported by corresponding reserve assets.

Reserve composition may include:

- Cash and cash equivalents
- Short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills
- Regulated money market funds including:
  - Franklin Templeton BENJI
  - Superstate USTB

Reserve assets are held in segregated accounts and are subject to monthly third-party attestations to support transparency and confidence in reserve integrity.

## Token Mechanics

### Issuance

USDR is minted 1:1 against USD reserves through regulated infrastructure.

### Distribution & Transfers

DollarCoin is distributed through Stables LLC and may be transferred across supported blockchain networks with near-instant settlement.

### Redemption

USDR is redeemable through approved channels for:

- U.S. Dollars (USD)
- USD Coin (USDC)
